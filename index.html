Nexus is a content-model visual builder. It reconstructs a Drupal site's content model - its entities, fields and relationships - from exported configuration, and lets you explore and edit it as an interactive diagram.

Everything runs in your browser. Any configuration you import and every diagram you build are kept in this browser only - there is no backend, and nothing you provide is uploaded to or processed by any server.
