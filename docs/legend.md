# Legend

The **Legend** panel is the key to the open diagram's symbols. Keep it open while you learn the [visual language](visual-language.md), or when you share your screen with someone new to Nexus.

## Open the legend

Click **Legend** in the toolbar, or press `3`. [Getting started](getting-started.md#arrange-the-panels) explains how to move, dock and resize a panel.

## What the legend lists

The legend lists these symbols, top to bottom:

1. Each entity type in the diagram, drawn in the color and shape your [settings](settings.md#entity-types) give it, including custom types.
2. The field symbols: **Single-value field**, **Multi-value field**, **System field** and **Calculated field**.
3. The annotation symbols: **Event**, **API**, **Callback / method** and **Note**.

While you [compare 2 versions](comparing.md), the legend also lists the 3 colors of a comparison: **Added**, **Removed** and **Changed**.
